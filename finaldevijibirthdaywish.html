Dear Devi Ji.

Happy Birthday, Boss.

Pata nahi tumhe long emotional messages pasand aate hain ya nahi, especially jab koi tumhari tareef karne lage. Toh tension mat lo, aaj tumhe awkward karne ka koi plan nahi hai.

Bas itna kehna hai ki tum apne aap mein ek alag hi insaan ho. Strong, independent, apni marzi ki malik. Aur us serious personality ke peeche chhupi hui woh cute si side bhi, jo kabhi-kabhi Shinchan Paglu ban jaati hai.

Tumhari zindagi mein pahaadon jaisa sukoon ho, tumhari favourite coffee jaisi warmth ho, aur gossip ke woh moments hon jo bina kisi effort ke tumhare chehre par smile le aayein.

Coffee tumhare liye sirf coffee nahi hai — woh tumhara Amrit hai. Toh meri birthday wish mein ek cheez pakki hai: tumhari coffee kabhi thandi na ho, aur tumhara sukoon kabhi kam na ho.

May you always have the courage to choose yourself, the freedom to live life your way, and the peace of knowing that you don't have to figure everything out all at once.

Radha-Krishna ka prem aur Shiv Ji ka aashirwad hamesha tumhare saath rahe. Tumhari smile bani rahe — bina kisi pressure ke, bas jab dil se aaye.

May life take you to beautiful mountains, give you countless reasons to laugh, people who understand your silence, and moments where you can simply be yourself.

No pressure to be perfect. No pressure to always be strong. Bas apni wali Devi Ji rehna.

Aur haan, Boss toh hamesha rahogi hi. Bas apne andar ke Shinchan Paglu ko kabhi retire mat karna.

With warmth, always. 🦚

Happy Birthday, Devi Ji.

Coffee strong. Mountains majestic. Gossip spicy. Faith steady. Heart peaceful.
